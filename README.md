# Beats by Dre Product Analytics

# Project Overview

## Overview
This project analyzes **consumer sentiment and product performance** for the BeatsByDre products; I specifically focused on the Beats Studio Pro Headphones (analyzing 1000+ Amazon reviews) compared against similar products from nine competing brands, including Sony and JBL.

The goal is to uncover **consumer behavior patterns, market trends, and actionable product insights** by examining customer reviews, ratings, and product attributes, which aims to highlight strengths and areas for improvement that can inform product development and marketing strategies.

---

## Focus
- Exploratory data analysis (EDA) of customer reviews and ratings  
- Gemini AI API to streamline querying and insight generation on sentiment
- Conduct sentiment analysis to identify strengths and pain points  
- Comparative benchmarking across competing headphone brands  
- Insight generation to inform product development and marketing strategy
